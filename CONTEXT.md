# Photography

Photographs are organized by the trips on which they were taken.

## Language

**Photo**:
A photograph in the public photography collection. A photo belongs to at most one trip, and trip membership is optional.

**Camera**:
The camera model used to take an individual photo, supplied by Ajan. Camera information belongs to the photo independently of its trip.

**Trip**:
A named journey that groups its photos for visitors to browse together. Separate visits to the same destination are separate trips.
_Avoid_: Album, tag

**Trip cover**:
The photograph representing a trip in the trip index.

**Trip index**:
The photography landing page, where visitors choose a trip by its cover and open its gallery.

**Unorganized**:
Photos with no assigned trip. They remain publicly visible in a separate collection after the trips, labeled "Other photographs" for visitors.

**Trip gallery**:
The photos belonging to one trip, shown together after a visitor opens that trip.
